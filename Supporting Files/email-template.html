Introducing Image Synchronization
 

Seamlessly synchronize product images between Business Central and WooCommerce in both directions

This feature uploads images from Business Central to WooCommerce product galleries and downloads images from WooCommerce into item and variant records in Business Central. Synchronization can run manually or automatically, handles image metadata, avoids duplicates, and logs all actions for traceability.

Note:

The image synchronization feature is only available as a separately purchased module. 

Key details

Supports both push operations from Business Central to WooCommerce and pull operations from WooCommerce to Business Central for image data.

Maintains consistent and current images for items and item variants.

Eliminates manual image handling to reduce errors and save time, especially when managing large product catalogs.

Delivers a consistent visual experience across platforms while streamlining internal image management workflows.

Scales to support growing catalogs, from small shops to large retailers with thousands of items.
