# Item description synchronization

By default, during item synchronization, the WooCommerce Connector updates the item descriptions in your webstore with the item descriptions from Business Central. If required, you can configure the WooCommerce Connector to retain the existing webstore item descriptions while continuing to synchronize other data, such as prices and inventory.

## To retain existing webstore item descriptions during item synchronization

1. Choose ![Lightbulb that opens the Tell Me feature.](media/ui-search/search_small.png "Tell me what you want to do"), enter <i>woocommerce connector setup</i>, and then choose the related link.

   The <b>WooCommerce Connector Setup</b> page opens.

1. On the <b>Posting</b> FastTab, turn on <b>Disable Description Sync</b>.
