# Synchronize item attributes

The WooCommerce Connector app provides the capability to synchronize item attributes and their values between WooCommerce and Business Central. Synchronizing ensures that your items are up-to-date and consistent between both platforms. 

When you perform a synchronization, the WooCommerce Connector synchronizes items based on the setup on the <b>WooCommerce Connector Setup</b> page.

## To synchronize selected item attributes

1. Choose ![Lightbulb that opens the Tell Me feature.](media/ui-search/search_small.png "Tell me what you want to do"), enter <i>woocommerce connector setup</i>, and then choose the related link.

   The <b>WooCommerce Connector Setup</b> page opens.

1. On the action bar, choose <b>Data</b> > <b>Webstore Item Attribute Mapping</b>.

   The <b>Webstore Item Attribute Mapping</b> page opens.

1. Select the item attribute that you want to synchronize, and then on the action bar, choose <b>Synchronization</b> > <b>Synchronize Selected</b>.

   A dialog box appears that displays the following text:

   <i>Do you want to synchronize selected item attribute with the webstore now?</i>

1. To proceed, choose <b>Yes</b>.

   The synchronization process completes and the <b>Webstore ID</b> field is populated for all records that were successfully mapped.
