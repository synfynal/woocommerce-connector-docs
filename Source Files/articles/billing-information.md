# Billing information

The following sections include information about billing.

## View your billing information

You can view your WooCommerce Connector billing information from the <b>Customer Portal</b>.

### To view your billing information

1. Choose ![Lightbulb that opens the Tell Me feature.](media/ui-search/search_small.png "Tell me what you want to do"), enter <i>woocommerce connector setup</i>, and then choose the related link.

   The <b>WooCommerce Connector Setup</b> page opens.

1. On the action bar, choose <b>Subscription</b> > <b>Customer Portal</b>.

## Partner billing

To ensure that end clients don't have to pay for the WooCommerce Connector directly, partners can specify their credit card details and billing information when upgrading a client's production environment to a paid subscription.

## Synchronizing over 500 sales orders per month

There is no additional cost for synchronizing more than 500 sales orders per month. The <b>Heavy Sync</b> price of $250.00 USD remains the same regardless of the number of orders beyond the 500-order threshold.

## Subscription cancellation timing

You can cancel your WooCommerce Connector subscription at any time. The cancellation will take effect after the billing period for the month in which you cancel ends.

## Supported payment methods for the subscription

Typically, customers use credit cards to pay for their subscription. Other payment methods, such as ACH credit transfer, are also available. To discuss alternative payment methods, feel free to <a href="https://www.synfynal.com/contact-us" target="_blank">contact us</a>.
