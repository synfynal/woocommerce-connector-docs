# Pricing

The WooCommerce Connector is free to use in a sandbox environment and includes a 15-day free trial in a production environment. After the trial ends, a subscription is required. Pricing is based on the number of sales orders that are synchronized per month. You only pay for what you use.

To learn more, go to <a href="https://www.synfynal.com/plans-pricing" target="_blank">Pricing</a>.

You can also purchase additional modules to extend the WooCommerce Connector's functionality. For details, go to [Additional paid features](additional-paid-features.md).
